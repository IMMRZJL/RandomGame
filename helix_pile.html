UWASP-22 Helical Pile Capacity Calculator (Single Helix)

Calculates approximate axial tension and compression capacities using the UWASP-22 CPT-based method. This page is designed to show every step and the fixed parameters used (230, 0.15, 0.20).

Step 1: Enter pile geometry and CPT inputs

Used in shaft term: qs = qc,s / 230
Used in helix term (tension): Qh = 0.15 qc,avg Ah
Used in helix term (compression): Qh = 0.20 qc,avg Ah
1 MPa = 1000 kPa, and kPa · m² = kN
The UWASP-22 summary describes averaging within 1 Dh of the helix in the direction of loading. Some users prefer symmetric averaging. Pick one and keep it consistent.
Accepts comma, tab, or space separation. Lines starting with # are ignored.
Auto-averaging method: trapezoidal integration (with linear interpolation at window boundaries), then average = area / window thickness.
Fixed parameters βc=230, αt=0.15, αc=0.20
